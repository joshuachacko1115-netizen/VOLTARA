    How the numbers are made

    SOH and RUL are gradient-boosted models. Their errors are measured on 20 % of batteries and vehicles held out before training and scored once. Manual entry uses a smaller model on age and equivalent full cycles (odometer × consumption ÷ capacity).

    Range is arithmetic: capacity × SOH × charge ÷ consumption. It is not a trained range model, and it is only as good as the consumption you enter.

    Anomalies combine statistical outlier flags (each reading against the battery's own recent readings) with fixed temperature, voltage and resistance thresholds. They need a history of readings.

    Insights are sentences written by fixed rules from the numbers above; no language model is involved. Scooters and buses are outside the training data and are labelled as extrapolation.

    VOLTARA v0.1 · models trained on two synthetic EV datasets · results are estimates, not a substitute for a capacity test.
